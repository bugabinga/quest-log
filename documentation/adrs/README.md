## What is an ADR?

An Architecture Decision Record (ADR) documents a significant architectural
decision - one that affects the structure, non-functional characteristics,
dependencies, interfaces, or build/live operations of the system.

ADRs are inspired by [MADR](https://adr.github.io/madr/) and follow their
format.
